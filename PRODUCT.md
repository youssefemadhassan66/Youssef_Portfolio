# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Primary: hiring managers and tech recruiters** evaluating Youssef for developer roles. They skim on a laptop between other candidates, often in under a minute, looking for stack fit, real shipped systems, and seniority signals.
- **Secondary: teams and founders** who need a developer to build a web app, dashboard or API.

Job to be done: decide within one scroll whether to download the CV, open a repo, or send a message.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio for Youssef Emad Hussin, a full-stack developer (Node.js, React, .NET, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB). It replaces a previous 3D "space" portfolio that looked impressive but said little. Success means a visitor leaves knowing what he builds, how well, and how to reach him.

## Brand Personality

Bold, sharp, ambitious. Big confident type, short punchy copy, every claim tied to a real project. Never hype without evidence.

Visual reference chosen by Youssef: the bazil.fr hero. Condensed heavy display type with an outlined second line, a black-and-white cutout portrait overlapping the type, light body text, black buttons, monochrome with one small accent dot.

## Anti-references

- The previous version of this site: starfields, neon gradients on black, glowing 3D objects, custom cursors, "space" theatrics.
- Decorative icons next to labels and links (locks, checkmarks, social glyphs, arrows). They read as AI-generated; use words instead.
- Positioning him as an e-commerce or store specialist, or naming client products (for example the socks sold by a client).
- Generic developer-portfolio templates: typing-animation role rotator, skill progress bars with percentages, identical icon-card grids.
- SaaS-landing clichés: hero metric strips, gradient text, glassmorphism cards.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over adjectives.** Every project shows stack, scope, and what it does. No "passionate developer" filler.
2. **Developer first.** The site sells a full-stack developer. A project's business domain is context, never the headline.
3. **Skimmable in one pass.** A recruiter scanning top to bottom should get role, stack, best work, and contact without clicking.
4. **Text over icons.** Labels, links and buttons are words. The only graphic marks are the ones from the reference hero.
5. **Honest about private work.** Private and client projects are clearly labeled, with no dead links.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, sufficient contrast in both light and dark themes, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, touch targets at least 44px on mobile.
